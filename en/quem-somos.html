Our history

A trajectory built on experience, precision and evolution.

Founded in 1988 in Joinville (SC), Boreal Plating has built its trajectory through the continuous evolution of its structure, processes and areas of operation. Today, it brings together experience and technical knowledge to deliver industrial solutions in surface treatment, machining and component manufacturing, with a focus on quality, precision and process control.
